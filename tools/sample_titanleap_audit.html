The Leaks

No fluff, no 12-point checklist dump. Here are the 3 things actually bleeding revenue, biggest first.

01

Dead CTA buttons

Homepage · pricing page · primary offer CTA
High

Your primary call-to-action button either goes nowhere, throws a 404, or lands on a page that can't convert. Visitors who reach a CTA already have intent — killing that moment costs full addressable revenue, not a fraction of it.

Est. monthly loss
$1,604–$3,742
Assumes ~500 monthly visitors, 3% reaching CTA intent, $297 offer price. At 60% recovery this leak is worth $1,604–$3,742/mo.
Fix

Audit every CTA link across homepage, pricing page, and any linked landing page. Fix broken URLs, confirm checkout loads, verify mobile tap targets aren't overlapping.

1–4 hrs · This week
02

Missing retargeting pixel

Site-wide — head tag
Med

No pixel means every visitor who bounces is gone forever — you can't retarget, lookalike, or attribute. Paid traffic you've already bought is single-use instead of a compounding asset.

Est. monthly loss
$267–$624
Assumes ~500 monthly visitors, 3% reaching CTA intent, $297 offer price. At 10% recovery this leak is worth $267–$624/mo.
Fix

Install Meta Pixel (or Google Tag) via GTM. Add purchase and lead events on thank-you pages so paid campaigns can optimise toward real buyers, not just clicks.

2–4 hrs · This week
03

Broken social links

Footer · header nav social icons
Med

Social links resolve to placeholder # or a 404. Every visitor who clicks to vet you hits a dead end. Trust holes like this don't just lose that visitor — they poison the whole purchase decision.

Est. monthly loss
$134–$312
Assumes ~500 monthly visitors, 3% reaching CTA intent, $297 offer price. At 5% recovery this leak is worth $134–$312/mo.
Fix

Update every social href to the live profile URL. Open each link in an incognito window to confirm it resolves. Remove any platforms you're not actually active on — a missing link is cleaner than a broken one.
