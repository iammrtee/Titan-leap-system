Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026. Applies to TitanLeap Monolith at monolith.titanleap.co ("the App"), operated by TitanLeap ("we", "us").

What the App is

The App is TitanLeap's business tool for auditing marketing, planning content and publishing posts to the social accounts that the signed-in user chooses to connect. Access is limited to authorised TitanLeap team members.

Information we collect

  • Account sign-in: the email address used to sign in.
  • Connected social accounts: when you connect Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X or TikTok, we receive an access token and basic profile details (account name and ID). For TikTok this is your display name, username and the posting options TikTok returns for your account (such as allowed privacy levels).
  • Content you create: captions, images, videos and schedules you upload to publish.
  • Business data you enter: prospect and lead details used for audits and outreach.

How we use it

Only to run the App: to publish the content you choose, to your connected accounts, at the time and with the privacy and interaction settings you select. For TikTok, we post only when you press Post and confirm, and only with the settings you chose. We do not sell personal information, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with third parties except the service providers needed to run the App (below).

Service providers

Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage), Render (hosting), Anthropic (AI text generation), and the social platforms you connect, such as TikTok, Meta, LinkedIn and X, when you publish to them.

Security

Access tokens for connected accounts are encrypted before they are stored, kept on the server, and never sent back to the browser. Connections use HTTPS.

Retention and deletion

You can disconnect any account at any time from Creatives → Connected Accounts, which deletes its stored tokens. You can also revoke TitanLeap's access from the platform's own settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services). To delete other data we hold about you, email info@titanleap.co and we will remove it within 30 days.
